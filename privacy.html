Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Astrocoders ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications, including Rentario. This policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard user information.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Rentario is engineered with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. Your property records, financial figures, tenant contacts, and tax notes are stored directly on your personal device using an encrypted local SQLite database. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal portfolio data.

2. Cloud Synchronization & Diagnostics

Where optional cloud sync or telemetry is enabled, data is transmitted over secure TLS encrypted connections:

  • Supabase: Provides optional database backup and user account authentication.
  • Sentry: Collects anonymous diagnostic crash telemetry and device specifications (OS version, device model) to help us diagnose bugs and prevent crashes.

3. Advertisements & Monetization

For users on free access tiers, our applications may integrate Google AdMob to display advertising. Google may use advertising IDs in accordance with its privacy policies to serve relevant advertisements. Premium subscribers enjoy a completely ad-free experience.

Subscription and in-app purchase verification is processed securely via RevenueCat in coordination with the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: astrocodersoficial@gmail.com.
