Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA, and Store Policies

SUMMARY: Rentario is built on a privacy-first, local-first architecture. Your property records, financial data, and tenant contacts reside securely on your personal device. We do not sell your personal data. We do not access your rental spreadsheets. Diagnostic error logs are strictly anonymized, and optional cloud backups use encrypted protocols.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights Guarantee ("LOPDGDD"), and applicable global privacy legislation, the Data Controller is:

Astrocoders

Contact Email: astrocodersoficial@gmail.com

2. What Information We Process (and What We Do NOT Collect)

A. User Portfolio & Rental Data (Stored Strictly Locally)

Rentario is engineered as a Local-First application. By default, all operational data entered into the Application is stored in an encrypted local SQLite database hosted exclusively on your personal device's sandbox. This includes:

  • Property names, addresses, cadastral notes, and unit descriptions.
  • Tenant names, lease dates, phone numbers, and nominal rental fees.
  • Payment ledgers, transaction records, expenses, and mortgage figures.
  • Generated PDF receipts and export dossiers.

Astrocoders does NOT have access to, does NOT monitor, does NOT harvest, and does NOT sell your local property or tenant database.

B. Diagnostic & Crash Telemetry (Sentry)

To detect software bugs, monitor crash rates, and ensure application stability across thousands of Android and iOS hardware configurations, Rentario integrates the Sentry SDK (provided by Functional Software, Inc., USA). When an unexpected crash or fatal exception occurs, the following technical diagnostics are transmitted:

  • Device manufacturer, model, and architecture (e.g., Google Pixel 8, Samsung Galaxy S24).
  • Operating system and version (e.g., Android 15, iOS 18).
  • Available RAM, battery level, network connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Application version, build number, and anonymized execution stack trace.
  • Internal navigation breadcrumbs (e.g., "Opened Reports Screen").

Sentry telemetry NEVER includes tenant contact books, property addresses, or monetary balances. Sentry processes data under standard contractual clauses (SCCs) ensuring high data security standards.

C. Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob)

For users on free access tiers, the Application integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA).
  • IP address (used to infer general coarse city/country location).
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and engagement metrics).

Users may manage, restrict, or reset their advertising identifiers directly through their Android or iOS device settings. Subscribers of Rentario Pro enjoy an entirely ad-free experience, where advertising SDK calls are suppressed.

D. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

When you purchase a subscription or unlock lifetime access, transactions are fulfilled directly by the Google Play Store (Google LLC) or Apple App Store (Apple Inc.). We integrate RevenueCat, Inc. to validate store purchase tokens and manage subscription entitlements.

  • Astrocoders never receives, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, debit cards, or bank billing details.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, product purchase token, and expiration timestamp.

E. Optional Cloud Sync & Authentication (Supabase)

If you choose to create an optional cloud account to back up your portfolio, authentication and cloud synchronization are processed through Supabase (Supabase, Inc.). Data transmitted to the cloud is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and protected at rest with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies tied strictly to your authenticated user ID.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

Under European data protection laws, we process technical information based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To deliver core application functionality, process in-app purchase entitlements, and execute cloud backups requested by you.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): To monitor software stability, fix bugs, prevent fraud, and maintain application security via anonymized diagnostic telemetry.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where required by law (such as serving personalized advertising in the EEA/UK), processing is based on your explicit consent gathered via consent management dialogues.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, lease, or commercialize your personal data to third parties. We share data only with trusted technical infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Application:

  • Google LLC (AdMob & Play Services): Advertising and app distribution. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry): Crash reporting and error diagnostics. Sentry Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: In-app purchase verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase, Inc.: Encrypted cloud database infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Device Data: All local records, properties, and tenant lists reside on your device. You can permanently delete all local data at any time by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling Rentario.
  • Cloud Data: If you created an optional cloud account, you may request complete account and data deletion at any time by emailing us at astrocodersoficial@gmail.com.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Sentry diagnostic logs are automatically purged after 90 days.

6. Your Rights Under the GDPR & International Law

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are entitled to exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation as to whether personal data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: Request temporary restriction of data processing under certain statutory circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your provided data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at: astrocodersoficial@gmail.com.

Right to Lodge a Complaint: You also have the right to lodge a formal complaint with a competent supervisory data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), accessible at www.aepd.es.

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. For any CCPA inquiries, contact us at astrocodersoficial@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Rentario is intended strictly for adults and professional or independent landlords aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
